privacy

Privacy and cookie policy for diskin.me.

This site uses cookies and third-party scripts to measure traffic and understand how visitors use its pages.

Analytics

The site loads the following services when you visit, without a cookie consent dialog:

  • Google Analytics
  • Yandex.Metrika

Google Analytics measures traffic, popular pages, and visitor interactions. Yandex.Metrika also has Webvisor session replay, click maps, link tracking, and bounce tracking enabled. The Yandex counter is configured with a dataLayer for ecommerce events. These services may set cookies and receive information about your browser, the page URL, and the referring page. Browser privacy settings and content blockers can restrict their requests and cookies.

Search Engine Verification

This site may also use ownership-verification meta tags for services such as Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. These tags are only used to verify control of the domain and do not provide visitor-facing functionality.
