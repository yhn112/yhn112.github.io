## Overview

Mentored students in the Practical Reinforcement Learning course, a joint offering by HSE University and Yandex originally published on Coursera ([HSE course page](https://elearning.hse.ru/moocs/practical-rl)). Covered policy gradient methods, Q-learning, actor-critic architectures, and practical applications.
