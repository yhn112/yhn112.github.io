### Long

Michael Diskin is Head of LLM R&D at Wildberries, Russia's largest e-commerce platform, and a researcher at HSE University.
Since 2024, he has built the company's LLM and embeddings organization from scratch,
growing it to 30+ people in 4–5 teams and running a 500+ GPU cluster.
His teams ship universal text embeddings for retrieval and ranking,
machine translation of tens of millions of product listings into 10+ languages,
RAG-powered assistants, and optimized LLM serving that cut GPU costs by more than 40%.

His research covers decentralized and communication-efficient training, LLM reasoning for mathematics,
and efficient, low-resource language models, with papers at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, EMNLP workshops, and WMT.
He co-created Hivemind, an open-source library for decentralized deep learning,
and, at Yandex Research, co-authored a widely cited benchmark for graph neural networks under heterophily.
Earlier roles include R&D on lip-sync models for AI video dubbing at Brask AI and computer vision research at Huawei.

He was responsible for the 2025 edition of the Yandex School of Data Analysis NLP course
and lectures there and at Harbour.Space University.
He holds an MSc in Computer Science from HSE University, awarded summa cum laude.
